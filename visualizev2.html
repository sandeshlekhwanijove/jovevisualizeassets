Lipid-Mediated Transfection of HEK293T

⏱️ Time: 3 Days
🌡️ Temp: 37°C
🧪 Materials: Lipofectamine 3000, Opti-MEM
1
Cell Seeding Seed 2.5 x 10^5 HEK293T cells per well in a 6-well plate. Ensure 70-80% confluency at time of transfection.
2
Prepare Lipid-DNA Complex Dilute 2.5µg plasmid DNA in 125µL Opti-MEM. In separate tube, dilute 5µL Lipofectamine in 125µL Opti-MEM. Combine and incubate 15 mins.
⚠️ Critical: Do not vortex the complex. Mix gently by pipetting.
3
Incubation & Analysis Add complex dropwise to cells. Incubate for 48 hours before harvesting for Western Blot or PCR.

CRISPR-Cas9 Mediated Genome Editing in HEK293T Cells: A Comprehensive Review
Generated by JoVE Visualize • January 30, 2026
Abstract
The clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic repeats (CRISPR)-Cas9 system has revolutionized genomic engineering. This report synthesizes current methodologies for lipid-mediated transfection of Cas9-sgRNA plasmids into HEK293T cells, highlighting critical optimization parameters for maximal cleavage efficiency.
Introduction
Precise manipulation of the genome is essential for functional genomics. While Zinc Finger Nucleases (ZFNs) and TALENs provided early tools, they were limited by complex protein engineering requirements. CRISPR-Cas9 offers a programmable RNA-guided alternative that is both cost-effective and scalable.
