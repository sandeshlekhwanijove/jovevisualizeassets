Full protocol (JoVE)
Lipid-mediated transfection of HEK293T with CRISPR-Cas9
Complete experimental procedure from cell seeding through transfection and downstream analysis.

Procedure

~3 days 37°C Lipofectamine 3000, Opti-MEM
1
Cell seeding Seed 2.5×10⁵ HEK293T cells per well in a 6-well plate. Aim for 70–80% confluency at transfection.
Watch on JoVE
2
Prepare lipid–DNA complex Dilute 2.5 µg plasmid in 125 µL Opti-MEM; in another tube dilute 5 µL Lipofectamine in 125 µL Opti-MEM. Combine and incubate 15 min.
Critical: Do not vortex. Mix gently by pipetting.
Watch on JoVE
3
Transfection and analysis Add complex dropwise to cells. Incubate 48 h before harvest for Western blot or PCR.
Watch on JoVE
